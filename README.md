# Rituals – AI-Powered Skincare & Haircare App

## 📱 Overview

**Rituals** is a modern mobile application that provides **AI-powered personalized skincare and haircare solutions**. Users can take assessments, receive customized kits, browse popular products, and consult experts directly within the app.

The app focuses on a clean UI, smooth onboarding experience, and personalization-driven recommendations.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* 🔍 **AI-Based Assessment** for skincare & haircare
* 🧴 **Personalized Kits** saved by assessment date
* 👨‍⚕️ **Expert Consultation** booking
* 🛍️ **Product Discovery & Shopping**
* 🔔 Alerts & profile management
* ⚡ Smooth splash screen & loading experience

---

## 🧭 App Navigation

* **Shop** – Browse best-selling and popular products
* **My Kit** – View saved personalized kits
* **Discover** – Explore recommendations
* **Alerts** – Notifications & updates
* **Profile** – User account & settings

---
